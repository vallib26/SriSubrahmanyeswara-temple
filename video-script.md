# Heritage Story Video — Script & Storyboard Template

Use this as a starting prompt/storyboard for any AI video generation tool once you've finalized the real story text in `index.html`. Fill in the [bracketed] parts with the real details your grandparents told.

## Video basics
- **Length:** 45–60 seconds
- **Format:** 1920×1080 (landscape), MP4
- **Tone:** Warm, reverent, gentle — like a memory being retold, not a dramatic trailer
- **Voiceover language:** [Telugu / English — pick one, or do both as two versions]

## Storyboard (6 scenes, ~8–10 seconds each)

**Scene 1 — Opening**
Visual: Soft sunrise light over the temple gopuram, gentle temple bells in background.
Voiceover: "This is the story of [Temple Name], as told by my grandparents."

**Scene 2 — The Beginning**
Visual: [Describe how the temple began — a modest shrine, a specific spot, a vision or event]
Voiceover: "[Describe in 1-2 sentences how it started — e.g. 'Long ago, in this very village, a small shrine stood where devotees gathered...']"

**Scene 3 — The Family's Role**
Visual: Old photographs (if available) or gentle illustration of the family's involvement.
Voiceover: "[What role did the grandparents/family play — building, protecting, serving the temple?]"

**Scene 4 — A Memory or Miracle**
Visual: Close-up of the deity, diya (lamp) flame, hands in prayer.
Voiceover: "[The specific anecdote/miracle/memory that's remembered — the heart of the story]"

**Scene 5 — Six Decades Later**
Visual: Present-day temple, devotees, daily pooja.
Voiceover: "Sixty years later, that same devotion continues here every single day."

**Scene 6 — Closing**
Visual: Temple exterior, fade to temple name/logo.
Voiceover: "[Closing line — an invitation, e.g. 'We invite you to be part of this living story.']"

## Notes for using an AI video tool
- Most AI video tools work best with one scene description at a time rather than the whole script at once — generate scene by scene, then stitch together in a free editor (CapCut, DaVinci Resolve are both free).
- If you have real old photographs from the grandparents' time, a simple Ken Burns-style pan/zoom over those (rather than fully AI-generated visuals) will usually feel more authentic for a story like this.
- Keep the voiceover as the real family's own words where possible — it's what will make this section feel genuine to devotees.
